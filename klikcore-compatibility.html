KlikCore compatibility

Games tested with KlikCore so far, and how well they run.

Download KlikCore About KlikCore

Last updated November 2026 · KlikCore 2.0.7 · Windows 11, Steam Deck and Android

What the ratings mean

Tested Played or put through automated play tests (real input, checked results), and every screen runs with nothing unsupported.

Runs Starts and every screen runs with nothing unsupported, but hasn't been played through yet.

Partial Runs, but uses something KlikCore doesn't support yet, or has known problems. The notes say what.

Not possible The game can't run on any Fusion runtime (for example a native port).

Every game below was loaded and had each of its screens run by KlikCore's compatibility sweep. A game that isn't listed hasn't been tried yet, and may well work. KlikCore plays your own copies of games and includes none of their files.

Tried a game?

If a game works, or doesn't, let me know on GitHub or by email. Each game writes a klikcore.log next to its saves (the launcher's Open folder button shows where); sending it along helps a lot.
